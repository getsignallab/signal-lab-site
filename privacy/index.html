Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Signal Lab ("we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Signal Lab is a marketing service for local businesses in Florida, run by Tony Guerrero. If you have any questions, contact us at getsignallab@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus basic data about how our website and ads are used:

  • Application form on our website: your name, website or Instagram, monthly revenue range, ad budget range, type of business, phone number, and email.
  • Facebook and Instagram lead forms: the details you submit, such as your name, email, and phone number.
  • Booking a call: your name, email, and the time you choose when you schedule through Cal.com.
  • Website activity: we use the Meta Pixel, which records things like pages viewed, form submissions, and bookings so we can measure and improve our ads. It may use cookies and information about your device and browser.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about your request, by phone, text, or email.
  • To schedule and hold calls with you.
  • To prepare proposals and provide our services.
  • To measure and improve our advertising and website.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use a few trusted services to run our business. They receive your information only to do their job for us:

  • Formspree, which processes our website form.
  • Cal.com and Google Calendar, which handle call bookings.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), for advertising, lead forms, and the Meta Pixel.
  • Netlify, which hosts our website.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you.
  • You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.
  • You can limit ad tracking in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings, and block or clear cookies in your browser.

To make a request, email getsignallab@gmail.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to respond to you, provide our services, or meet legal obligations. Then we delete it.

Children

Our services are for business owners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and change the date at the top.
