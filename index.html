04Lead follow-upEnglish / Español

Every lead hears from you within minutes, in their language.

The follow-up script your leads will hear: a call and a text, in English and Spanish.

English

Call, within 5 minutes

“Hi [Name], this is [You] from [Business]. You just asked about [service] on Facebook. Do you have two minutes so I can get you on the schedule?”

Text, if no answer

“Hi [Name], it's [You] from [Business]. I just tried calling about your [service] request. What's a good time to talk today? You can also reply here.”

Español

Llamada, en menos de 5 minutos

“Hola [Nombre], le habla [Usted] de [Negocio]. Acaba de pedir información sobre [servicio] en Facebook. ¿Tiene dos minutos para agendar su cita?”

Mensaje, si no contesta

“Hola [Nombre], soy [Usted] de [Negocio]. Le acabo de llamar por su solicitud de [servicio]. ¿A qué hora le puedo llamar hoy? También puede responder por aquí.”
